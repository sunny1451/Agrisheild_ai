# 🌱 AgriShield AI

### Smart Farming. Smarter Future.

AgriShield AI is a smart agriculture platform designed to help farmers monitor and manage their farms using modern web technologies, IoT concepts, sensor data, automation, and AI-assisted insights.

The platform provides a centralized interface for monitoring farm conditions, managing water and irrigation, viewing alerts, analyzing farm data, and accessing intelligent recommendations.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

Agriculture depends heavily on factors such as soil moisture, temperature, humidity, water availability, irrigation, and timely decision-making.

AgriShield AI aims to provide farmers with a simple and accessible digital platform to monitor important farm conditions and make informed decisions.

The project combines:

- 🌱 Smart Farm Monitoring
- 💧 Water Management
- 🚰 Pump Control
- 🌡️ Environmental Monitoring
- 🤖 AI-Assisted Insights
- 📊 Farm Analytics
- 🚨 Smart Alerts
- 📱 Mobile Application
- 💻 Web Dashboard
- 🔐 User Authentication

---

## ✨ Features

### 🌱 Farm Monitoring

Monitor important farm parameters through a centralized dashboard.

- Soil moisture
- Temperature
- Humidity
- Water level
- Pump status
- Farm status

---

### 💧 Water Management

Monitor water availability and irrigation conditions.

The system can display water-level information and provide alerts when water availability becomes low.

---

### 🚰 Pump Management

AgriShield AI provides a digital interface for controlling the irrigation pump.

Users can:

- Turn the pump ON
- Turn the pump OFF
- Monitor pump status
- Receive safety alerts

The system can also implement safety logic to prevent unsafe pump operation when water levels become critically low.

---

### 🤖 AI Insights

The platform provides intelligent insights based on available farm data.

Examples include:

- Low soil moisture
- High temperature
- Low water level
- Abnormal sensor readings
- Irrigation-related recommendations
- Farm condition alerts

---

### 📊 Analytics

Farm data can be visualized through analytics dashboards.

Supported information can include:

- Soil moisture trends
- Temperature trends
- Humidity trends
- Water-level trends
- Pump runtime
- Alert statistics

---

### 🚨 Alerts

The system provides alerts for important farm conditions.

Examples:

- Low water level
- Low soil moisture
- High temperature
- Sensor abnormalities
- Pump safety conditions

---

### 🔧 Device Monitoring

The platform provides a centralized view of connected or simulated farm devices and sensors.

---

### 👤 User Account

Users can create and manage their AgriShield AI account.

Account functionality includes:

- Registration
- Login
- User profile
- Farm information
- Profile management
- Logout
